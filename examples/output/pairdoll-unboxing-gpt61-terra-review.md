# Sex Doll Unboxing Checklist: What to Check on Delivery Day

A large delivery can be exciting, but it also takes some preparation. Before opening the carton, clear enough space to work and have your order details ready. A careful inspection will help you identify missing items or concerns while the packaging is still available.

**Check and photograph the carton, unpack with care, support the doll as you move it, and compare the contents with your order. If anything concerns you, document it and contact the seller before cleaning or attempting a repair.**

Keep the instructions for your particular doll nearby. Handling, assembly, cleaning, and storage directions can differ by product.

## What should you prepare before the package arrives?

Review your order confirmation, including the options and accessories you selected. Read the delivery information the seller provided, and note whether any items are expected in separate packages.

Choose a space with room to open the carton and move around it. Clear the route from the entrance, paying attention to tight corners, rugs, and other obstacles. Prepare a clean, stable surface where you can place the doll and inspect separate components.

Have your order details, a camera, and a tool for opening tape within reach. You may also want a clean cloth to protect the prepared surface. If the package seems too heavy or awkward to handle comfortably, arrange help in advance. Check the supplied handling instructions and make sure anyone helping knows where the package will go.

## What should you check before you open the carton?

Look over the package before cutting the tape. Check for crushed corners, punctures, tears, moisture, or areas that appear to have opened during transit. A damaged carton does not necessarily mean its contents are damaged, but recording its condition gives you a useful reference.

Photograph the carton from several sides and take closer pictures of any areas that concern you. If you photograph the shipping label for your records, protect your address and other personal information when sharing images.

Compare the delivery details with your order. Keep the carton and protective materials until you have inspected the contents and resolved any concerns. The seller may ask you to refer to them or provide instructions for a return.

## How can you unpack and move the doll safely?

Place the carton where its flaps can open without blocking your path. Cut the tape carefully, keeping the blade shallow and away from the contents. Open the flaps slowly, then look at how the doll and any separate parts are arranged before removing the protective material.

Follow the product’s handling directions. If someone is helping you, agree on how you will lift the doll, which areas each person will support, and where you will set it down. Keep the route clear and pause if either person cannot maintain a secure hold.

Support the body as directed. Do not pull on the head, hands, feet, fingers, or other delicate parts, and avoid dragging the doll across the floor or another rough surface. If a component catches on the packaging, free the packaging instead of tugging on the component.

Do not force a limb into position or attach a part when the instructions are unclear. Consult the supplied guide or ask the seller about unfamiliar fittings.

## What belongs on the first inspection checklist?

Compare the contents with your order confirmation and any packing list. Check the doll, separate components, and accessories against what you ordered. Look through the protective materials before setting them aside, since small pieces can be easy to overlook.

Use good light and work through the following list:

- **Body and visible surfaces:** Look for tears, punctures, marks, or other areas that concern you.
- **Components and fittings:** Check that separate parts are present and inspect accessible attachment points without forcing them.
- **Selected options:** Compare visible features with the options on your order.
- **Accessories:** Check what arrived against your purchase details and the seller’s stated contents.

Reposition the doll only as far as the handling instructions allow. You do not need to test every possible position on delivery day. If something resists movement or looks unusual, stop and record what you see.

Describe observations without guessing at their cause. A clear photo and a description of a mark can help the seller assess it; you do not need to decide whether it can be repaired.

## What if something is missing or damaged?

Photograph the concern before changing anything. Take a wider picture to show its location and a closer one in good light. If the carton or protective material also appears damaged, keep those pictures with the images of the contents. Preserve the original files.

Contact the seller with your order number, a concise description, and the relevant photos. If an item is missing, explain what you expected and list what arrived. First check whether the seller said it would ship separately. Ask what information the seller needs and what you should do next.

Do not apply glue, clean the affected area, or attempt another repair before receiving guidance. These actions can change what the seller needs to assess. Keep the packaging and any loose pieces together while you wait, and follow the seller’s directions for documenting or returning the item.

## When should you clean and set up the doll?

Complete the initial inspection first. If you have reported a concern, ask the seller whether to wait before cleaning or assembling the affected area.

For routine care, read the instructions for your product and its materials. Use the cleaning methods and products recommended in those directions. Do not assume an oil, solvent, adhesive, or heat tool is suitable because it appeared in a general guide.

Set up the doll on a clean, supported surface, following the supplied assembly and storage guidance. If the instructions are missing or leave a practical question unanswered, contact the seller or manufacturer before experimenting. Keep the care guide with your order records so you can find it later.

## Conclusion

A thorough delivery-day check starts with the carton and continues through unpacking and inspection. Document concerns, compare everything with your order, and pause when something does not look right. Once your questions are resolved, follow the product-specific directions for cleaning and setup.

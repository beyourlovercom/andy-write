# Sex Doll Unboxing Checklist: What to Check on Delivery Day

The delivery truck has gone, and the box you've been waiting for is finally in your home. Before cutting the tape, give yourself a few minutes to get organized. A full-size doll is not an ordinary parcel: it can be awkward to move, and some of its smaller parts may be tucked into separate protective bags.

**Start with three things: a clear place to unpack, your order confirmation, and a way to photograph the packaging. Check the box and its contents before making adjustments or starting a cleaning routine.** That short inspection gives you a much clearer picture of what arrived and makes any conversation with the seller easier.

This guide follows the journey from doorstep to first setup. It focuses on observations you can make without risking damage. Exact accessories, joint designs, care methods, and return procedures vary by model and seller, so keep your product instructions close by.

## What should you prepare before delivery day?

Pull up the order confirmation and find the specific choices you made: the body and head combination, material, skin tone, any removable inserts, and paid accessories. A product gallery can show options that weren't part of your order. Your invoice or order summary is the better checklist.

Save the shipping notification and the seller's messages together with the order details. The [FTC's online-shopping guidance](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/online-shopping) recommends keeping records such as receipts and seller communications. You don't need to print everything; a folder on your phone is enough, provided you can find it when the carton arrives.

Next, measure the space you'll use. A clean floor area or a wide, stable surface is easier to work on than the edge of a crowded bed. Clear a route from the front door and place a soft, light-colored sheet where the doll can rest. If the package is too large to move comfortably, plan for a second person or suitable moving equipment rather than improvising once it is on the doorstep.

## What should you photograph before opening the box?

Begin with the shipping carton while it is still closed. Photograph the label for your private records, then take wider pictures of each side. Pay attention to crushed corners, punctures, damp patches, retaped seams, or anything that suggests the carton has been handled roughly.

**A damaged carton does not automatically mean the doll inside is damaged.** The point of these photographs is to preserve what you actually received, not to decide what happened in transit. If the packaging looks fine, a few ordinary pictures are still useful because they show the starting condition.

Open the tape slowly and keep blades away from the contents. Packaging varies, but the original owner guide describes foam lining, protective wrapping around the body, a bagged head, and padding around vulnerable areas such as hands and feet. Your shipment may be packed differently. Photograph the arrangement before removing everything, and put each protective piece to one side rather than throwing it out immediately.

## How can you take the doll out safely?

The most awkward moment may be moving the body from the carton to the inspection area. Before lifting, check the stated weight and the manufacturer's handling instructions. The shape matters as much as the number on the shipping label: a long, uneven load can be difficult to grip and balance.

The [CDC/NIOSH guidance on ergonomics](https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/ergonomics/about/index.html) identifies heavy or awkward lifting and unnatural postures as risk factors for injury. If you cannot move the doll comfortably, ask another adult for help or use appropriate equipment. Keep the path clear and avoid sudden twisting movements.

**Do not pull the doll out by the head, wrists, hair, fingers, or any attachment that isn't specifically designed for lifting.** Support the body according to the model's instructions. The original reference article describes a particular way of bending the hips and lifting the body, but that is not a universal method. Different skeletons and joints can have different ranges of motion. Never force a joint to reach an angle simply because a guide suggests it.

Once the doll is on a stable, cushioned surface, remove the remaining wrapping carefully. There is no prize for rushing this step; a supported inspection is much easier than trying to identify a problem while holding the weight.

## What should you inspect before you discard any packaging?

Work through the order in a consistent sequence: identify the major components, check visible surfaces, and then compare the accessories. That keeps you from repeatedly moving a heavy body to look for the same details.

| Check | What to look for | What to record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Model and options | Body, head, skin tone, selected inserts or features | Order line item and a clear photo |
| Surface condition | Visible cuts, tears, unusual marks, deformation, or damaged makeup | A wider image plus a close-up |
| Hands and feet | Fingertips, toes, exposed wires or visibly damaged areas | Photograph the exact location |
| Joints and connectors | Obvious looseness, misalignment, or damage visible during permitted handling | Describe what you observed; do not force movement |
| Accessories | Items included in your specific order, including separately packed parts | Lay them out and compare with the confirmation |

Some accessories may be inside small fabric bags or tucked along the foam supports. Check all compartments before deciding something is missing. An item shown in promotional photography is not necessarily included with every configuration.

You can also look for anything that doesn't match the agreed appearance. Be careful about how you describe it: “a tear is visible near the shoulder” is an observation; “the carrier dropped the package” is an explanation you may not be able to prove. ## What should you do if something is missing or damaged?

First, stop adjusting the affected area. Take one photograph that shows where the concern is on the doll, followed by a close-up with the detail clearly visible. Keep the original files. A short written note — what you noticed and when — will help when you contact the seller.

Do not attempt to hide, glue, heat, stretch, or clean away a suspected defect before asking for instructions. Repairs or aggressive cleaning can change the condition you are trying to document. Keep the carton, inner supports, protective bags, and other packaging while the issue is unresolved.

Send the seller a concise message with your order number, the specific component involved, a neutral description, and the relevant photos. Ask how they want you to proceed. Shipping claims, replacement procedures, and return conditions are seller- and location-specific, so avoid assuming that another owner's experience will apply to your order.

If everything looks right, you can move on without having to document each square inch. The purpose of an arrival-day check is confidence and clarity, not a complicated inspection ritual.

## How should you handle the first cleaning and setup?

Once the inspection is complete, identify the doll's material and read its model-specific care instructions. The original reference guide includes a long maintenance-kit list, but not every suggestion is appropriate for every material, surface finish, adhesive, electrical feature, or removable insert.

Start with the basics that the manufacturer actually approves: a suitable soft cloth, a clean place to dry components, and any recommended cleaning product. Whether a part may be rinsed, submerged, or removed depends on its construction. A removable insert can have different care requirements from the body itself.

**Avoid treating oil, powder, strong cleaners, heat guns, repair glue, or improvised tools as universal first-day essentials.** They may be appropriate for a particular product under specific instructions, but the wrong treatment can damage another. If the manual is unclear, ask the seller before applying anything to a visible or intimate-use surface.

Finally, choose a storage position that supports the doll and avoids unnecessary pressure on delicate areas. Check the manufacturer's guidance for joints, long-term positioning, wigs, and detachable components. You'll have plenty of time to learn the routine; delivery day is simply the time to confirm that the product arrived safely and is ready for the next step.

## Conclusion

A useful unboxing checklist doesn't need to be complicated. Prepare a clean space, save your order details, photograph the carton, lift with appropriate support, inspect the components, and resolve any visible problems before cleaning or changing anything.

The one rule worth remembering is simple: **document what arrived, then follow the instructions for the exact doll you own.** That protects both the product and your ability to explain a genuine problem clearly.

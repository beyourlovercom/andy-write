# Sex Doll Unboxing Checklist: What to Check on Delivery Day

A delivery-day inspection helps you decide whether to proceed with setup or pause while a problem is reviewed. You do not need to test every feature at once. Start by checking the carton, comparing the contents with your order, and looking for visible damage.

**Keep the packaging until the inspection is complete.**

Follow the instructions supplied with your product for handling, assembly, and care.

## What should you prepare before the package arrives?

Have your order confirmation available so you can check the selected options and listed accessories. Note any delivery or inspection deadlines in the seller’s terms, and plan enough time to look over the contents.

Clear a route from the entrance to the unpacking area. Choose a clean, stable surface with enough space for the carton and its contents. A bed or sturdy table might work, provided it can support the load and allows you to reach the package safely.

If the carton may be heavy or awkward, arrange help before it arrives. A suitable hand truck might help on a clear route. The [CDC’s materials-handling guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/publications/numbered/2026-119.html) discusses planning for lifting and carrying.

Keep a phone or camera nearby, along with the seller’s order details. You can choose cleaning supplies after checking the instructions for the doll’s material.

## What should you check before you open the carton?

Confirm that the delivery label identifies the package as yours. Then look at all accessible sides for punctures, crushed corners, torn seams, or wet areas.

If you find damage, photograph the whole carton and take closer pictures that show where the damage is located. A puncture on the outside does not necessarily mean the contents are damaged, but the photographs can help establish what you received.

Shipping labels may contain personal information. Share an unedited label only through an appropriate private channel if it is needed to identify the order.

Keep the carton and packing materials during inspection. A shipment might contain foam, a fabric drawstring bag, or other protection; their presence should not be treated as a universal packing requirement. Set materials aside carefully and check them for small items before deciding anything is missing.

## How can you unpack and move the doll safely?

Move the carton to the unpacking area while it is still closed, if you can do so safely. Get help with a load that is too heavy or awkward to control, and avoid a route where you could trip or be forced to change your grip suddenly.

Place the carton in a stable position. Cut the tape with a shallow blade, keeping it away from the contents beneath. Open the flaps and remove protection gradually so you can see how the body and any separate parts are positioned.

Follow the supplied handling directions. Support the body during a move; do not lift it by a finger, the head, or another small part. If wrapping catches, stop and free it without pulling. If you cannot move the contents in a controlled way, pause and get another person to help.

## What belongs on the first inspection checklist?

Work in good light and compare what you can see with the order confirmation. Start with these checks:

- **Order:** Do the visible options and separate components match what you ordered?
- **Accessories:** Are the items listed in the confirmation present? Check the carton and packing material for small pieces.
- **Surfaces:** Are there visible cuts, tears, stains, or areas that look damaged?
- **Delicate areas:** Are the hands and fingers intact? Look closely without bending or pulling them to test them.
- **Components and fittings:** Is anything visibly loose, broken, or detached when the instructions say it should be fitted?

A mark or loose item may need clarification; appearance alone does not establish its cause. Photograph anything uncertain before cleaning or assembly. Do not force a joint or remove a fitted part to complete this first check.

## What if something is missing or damaged?

For a missing item, first compare its description with the order confirmation and look through the packing materials again. Record the exact listed item you cannot find. If it is required for assembly, leave that step unfinished until you know how to proceed.

For visible damage, pause setup and take an overall photograph followed by clear close-ups. Include the damaged carton in your record if relevant. Keep the original images and packaging, and send the seller your order information, a concise description, and the photographs through a private channel.

Do not glue, clean, or otherwise alter the affected area while it needs review. Ask about the applicable return or replacement process and any deadline before sending anything back or discarding packaging. If the body cannot be moved without putting pressure on the damaged area, leave it supported and seek handling guidance.

## When should you clean and set up the doll?

Proceed once the order check is complete and there is no unresolved concern that could be affected by cleaning or assembly. Read the manufacturer’s directions for the specific doll before choosing products or attaching components.

Material matters. Do not assume a cleaning product or storage method suitable for one TPE or silicone product is suitable for another. Follow the supplied instructions for cleaning, drying, and any surfaces or openings that need special treatment. Avoid improvising with solvents, oils, heat tools, or glue.

Prepare a supported work area and use only the supplies the instructions recommend. During assembly, check the orientation of each part before applying pressure. If a component does not fit as described or a joint meets resistance, stop rather than forcing it; obtain clarification before continuing.

## Conclusion

A useful inspection ends with a decision. If the contents match your order and show no concerns, continue with the supplied setup and care instructions. If something is missing, damaged, or unclear, document it, preserve the packaging, and pause the affected step until you know what to do next.

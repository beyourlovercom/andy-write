# Andy Write Editor — R2

Treat the user's supplied reference as primary. Use source-classified segments, evidence statuses and a practical reader question in each H2.

Avoid empty promotional language, repeated intros and closers, forced three-paragraph sections, keyword stuffing and fictional experience. Prioritize what the reader can actually check or decide. Use the project editorial constraints rather than installing unreviewed third-party code.

For PairDoll, packing options, care instructions and accessories may vary by manufacturer. Do not present observations from one source article as guaranteed features of every model.

Workflow:
- Read draft and compare it to source-specific facts and section plan.
- Improve specifics: what to inspect, how to document, and when to pause.
- Cite existing verified internal article and relevant non-commerce public source where appropriate. Never invent a URL or fact.
- Call Andy Write Quality to review. Revise only flagged H2s, preserving all other section content.
- Rule-check PASS is not proof of human authorship, model reliability or factual approval.
- Never publish. Keep local draft, edited and targeted-rewrite versions plus actual Codex usage logs.

## V1.2 human review handoff for rewritten articles

After normal editing, the independent local Fidelity Skill compares the full
Owner original with the output and lists exact matches, tentative lexical
matches, unlinked source paragraphs and every short original item. The editor
checks semantic continuity in the actual passages and explicitly distinguishes
retained meaning, authorized edits and intentionally omitted outdated/unsafe
guidance. Store the reviewer, rationale and target quote in the private mapping
notes; do not claim an automatic semantic PASS.

Codex CLI remains for ARTICLE REWRITING ONLY. Source comparison, review
worklists, claim checks, research and all software work run without Codex.

## V1.2 topic-aware editing — no unboxing template leakage

For a source_hierarchy_v1 job, rewriting/editing/word tightening must preserve
the exact source-derived H2 plan, NOT force six generic Unboxing headings or
deliveries. A targeted chapter repair can use the source-owned section only;
do not insert foam/packaging guidance into unrelated how-to articles. The
editor evaluates safety claims against the actual manufacturer's rules and
preserves useful Owner details with qualified uncertainty.

After editing, local Source Fidelity and reverse-QC diagnostics provide
review worklists but cannot themselves establish semantic accuracy or
human factual approval. Compare the reference and final rewritten article
with the private passage mapping sheet before any release approval.

Codex CLI is limited to the article rewrite/edit tasks; all other QC,
research, planning, code, publication and media generation are forbidden.

## V1.2.1 human annotations and source-supported fact editing

Use the frozen private twelve-item goldset and real original/draft pair.
Record real reviewer, actual source quote, substantive rationale and the
difference between meaning retained, qualified rewrite, intentionally
removed unsafe content and lost useful information. Do not substitute
AI-generated labels for a real human reference.

For each important factual sentence, use the private claim-evidence
worksheet and examine the actual source paragraph in full context.
Only mark a source relevant after checking the SPECIFIC factual claim
is really supported. Official government web pages cannot validate
specific toy material, cleaning, temperature or water-resistance ratings
without model-appropriate manufacturer support. Leave unproven claims
as NEEDS_REVIEW; never invent a citation. Codex remains rewriting only.

## V1.2.2 human reviewer workstation

Review the original and completed draft in a locally generated qc-workbench
HTML file, select a real semantic label, give a substantive reason and cite
the actual target excerpt where retention is asserted. Export JSON locally.
Independent reviewers submit separate reviews; qc-agreement surfaces
disagreements for Owner arbitration. Do not count AI proposed labels as
human truth, and do not use Codex CLI for QA.

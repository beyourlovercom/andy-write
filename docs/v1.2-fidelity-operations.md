# Andy Write V1.2 — Article Fidelity / Content Retention Review

Owner P0: Skill-first, evidence-driven and rollback-safe. This checker does not use Codex CLI. No image/video generation.

## Run

The original must stay in private, Git-ignored .runs:

    python3 -m andy_write fidelity \
      --source .runs/pairdoll-unboxing/source-original.notion.md \
      --draft examples/output/pairdoll-unboxing-editor-quality-v2.md \
      --report .runs/pairdoll-unboxing/fidelity-current.json

Output: machine-readable JSON plus a private .review.md worksheet. Both stay in .runs. Standard Codex and optional API writing flows automatically produce source-fidelity-report.json and source-fidelity-review.md AFTER the rewriting call, using local Python only.

## Meaning of statuses

- EXACT_TEXT_PRESENT: original paragraph visibly occurs in draft, after formatting normalization. This is not a factual quality guarantee.
- LEXICAL_CANDIDATE: an overlapping paragraph window includes enough distinctive source words to warrant human comparison. It does not prove the meaning survived.
- UNMAPPED_REVIEW: automatic word-level matching could not locate a convincing candidate. This is not proof the article omitted the meaning.
- SHORT_OR_HEADING_REVIEW: small original item is tracked by paragraph number/hash rather than silently removed. It requires manual review.
- Distinctive token presence is a lexical indicator, NOT a factual/semantic content-retention percentage.
- New numeric statements with units are review candidates, not automatically invented claims.
- All reports say HUMAN_REVIEW_REQUIRED until a real editor examines the original and target; no auto-pass for semantic fidelity.

## Manual decisions

Open both private documents, review every numbered source paragraph and the candidate passage, then record RETAINED, CHANGED_WITH_APPROVAL or INTENTIONALLY_OMITTED. Changes or removals may be legitimate, especially if original instructions are unsafe or not applicable to a destination SKU.

Optional private --notes file: JSON array containing source_id, source_sha256, reviewer, status, reason (at least 12 characters); positive approvals also require literal target_quote from the draft. Notes are reviewer assertions, not machine factual approval. The system verifies quote presence and source hash, but cannot independently prove the reviewer is the Owner.

## Safety

Do not maximize literal similarity at the cost of safety or product specificity. No original source, detailed report or review worksheet is committed to GitHub. No Codex calls for matching, research, QA or coding. No WordPress or Notion content is changed.

## Version

Contract andy-write-source-fidelity-v1.2. Any matcher-threshold change must preserve the two-topic positive identity cases, the controlled-deletion negatives, explicit human pending status and historical rollback.

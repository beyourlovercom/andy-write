## R3. Manufacturer-specific evidence proof: ENGINEERING COMPLETE

The claim evidence tool now refuses to mark a PRODUCT_OR_SAFETY_SPEC supported merely because reviewer boolean=true.

The actual cited and successfully retrieved source MUST be classified manufacturer_primary with matching HTTPS manufacturer domain, actual product model, official_product_manual or official_model_specifications document type, source quotation from cached paragraphs, and reviewer confirmation of that model. These fields are reviewer-declared; human validation of real manufacturer domain and actual manual provenance remains essential. No technical boolean is automatic factual proof.

## Four QC dimensions worth extracting, not copying

1. Structure and scan-readability: clear opening answer, actual source-derived H2/H3 hierarchy, useful decisions and conditional FAQ/table format. Old Andy Write forced six Unboxing sections on unrelated topics and flattened the original nested topics.
2. Evidence and citation validity: 59 internal and 16 external links do not establish that claims are proven. Some external domains appear retailer/vendor-related and must not become outgoing shopping links. Every claim needs an exact relevant source; HTTP 200 and a government-domain mention do not verify nearby claims.
3. Manufacturer and safety caveats: the KissSelf text includes waterproof rating, material compatibility and cleaning/disinfection advice. An offline language-pattern checker marks 49 of 109 paragraphs as REVIEW CANDIDATES, including 39 containing material/safety terms and 19 using absolute/universal phrasing (overlap applies). These are NOT 49 factual errors. Manufacturer-specific properties and real supporting references must be checked before advice is treated as verified.
4. Usefulness and original-source fidelity: action detail, conditions and exceptions matter more than word count. Extensive nested lists/quotes can be helpful or repetitive. Keep substantive Owner source details while allowing justified removal of outdated/unsafe advice. No AI detector percentage is a QC acceptance metric.

## Reusable Skills implemented

- Offline public WordPress reverse-QC extractor: bounded read-only REST capture, actual section/table/list/Alt/link metrics and factual-claim language risk candidates, without committing competitor prose.
- Offline qc-profile CLI: private WP snapshot vs existing Owner draft, with a brand URL provided explicitly; emits structural comparison and reusable Skill change tickets, not a false quality ranking.
- Source hierarchy mode: opt-in source_hierarchy_v1 builds H2/H3 plan from real owner/team Markdown source headings and validates the R1 source SHA-256. Original Unboxing mode is preserved for regression safety.
- Rewrite/Editor: the actual source-derived H2 plan is used for all drafting/editing/word-control stages. No fixed six-H2 rule or foam/packaging template on unrelated how-to topics.
- Quality: editorial-process language in reader headings and excessive H3 flattening produce review flags. Material/product claims are candidates for human evidence checks, not automatically judged false.
- 12-item human annotation goldset: deterministic private original-to-draft passage sample with source hashes, matching candidates, safety review priority, labeled options and explicit Owner confirmation PENDING. No automatic semantic score.
- Added cross-topic tests with public benchmark fixtures, read-only fetch and non-private-output denial, source-hierarchy behavior and editorial-leak regression checks.

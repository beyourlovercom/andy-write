## R1. New reusable human-label calibration protocol

New module andy_write/qc_calibration.py. Command: qc-calibrate.

- Source SHA-256, draft SHA-256, every paragraph ID/hash and all 12 original sampling positions are fixed before calibration.
- Reviewer record must include label, named independent reviewer, declaration of actually comparing source and draft, substantive reason and real excerpt from draft for positive retention.
- Reject altered sources, invented quotes, missing/duplicate labels, AI-automatic impersonation and invalid decisions. Reviewer declaration is recorded evidence, not cryptographic proof of identity.
- Separate retained meaning and retained-with-qualification from intentionally omitted outdated/unsafe advice. A justified omission is not the same as accidental lost useful information.
- Show baseline confusion matrix and exploratory threshold candidates ONLY if all twelve real human labels have enough positive and negative cases. Report missing prediction-class diversity separately.
- Do not auto-apply thresholds. Twelve samples cannot independently validate a model trained on those same examples; Owner release approval remains PENDING.

## R2. Important claims -> real source paragraphs -> human relevance

New module andy_write/claim_evidence.py. Command: qc-claims.

- Extract actionable, quantified, safety/material, compatibility and universal assertion candidates with stable claim IDs, original draft location and text hash; HIGH/MEDIUM priority.
- Separate Owner original reference (editorial provenance; NOT independently verified product truth) from actually retrieved external passages.
- External evidence requires URL, retrieval channel, actual source paragraph and body SHA-256. Do not mistake a successful HTTP response, page title or government-domain link for factual support.
- Detect topical overlaps, citation-to-passage mismatches, possible negation and numeric contradictions. These are MANUAL REVIEW CANDIDATES, never automatic entailment proof.
- Real reviewer annotations may mark SUPPORTED_BY_QUOTED_SOURCE, SOURCE_DOES_NOT_SUPPORT, SOURCE_CONTRADICTS_CLAIM, SOURCE_UNAVAILABLE, MANUFACTURER_SPEC_REQUIRED. Positive review requires the actual quoted passage from the recorded source plus reviewer attestation, rationale and exact claim hash.
- Materials, sanitation, cleaning, waterproofing and compatibility additionally require actual model/manufacturer-specific confirmation. Generic FTC or CDC pages are not sufficient.
- The final review and publishing gate stays closed even if structural and readability checks pass.
